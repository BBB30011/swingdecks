# DECISION QUALITY DEFINITION
Decision quality is strictly measured by:
1. TRACEABILITY
2. PIT INTEGRITY
3. EVIDENCE COMPLETENESS
4. DECISION REPRODUCIBILITY (Replay)
5. RISK GOVERNANCE
6. THESIS CONSISTENCY
7. EXECUTION RECONCILIATION
8. AUDIT COMPLETENESS

Profitability is classified as `POST_DECISION_OUTCOME` and is explicitly isolated from quality scoring.
