# SwingDeck Project Memory

This document is the durable ledger of the SwingDeck project. It tracks the North Star, architectural decisions, completed batches, validated findings, and current bottlenecks. 

## 1. The North Star
SwingDeck must transition from a simplistic momentum screener to a highly governed, evidence-based causal reasoning engine. It must answer **WHAT MOVED?**, **WHY DID IT MOVE?**, **WHO ELSE IS AFFECTED?**, and **IS THE CAUSATION SUPPORTED BY EVIDENCE?** before deciding to trade.

## 2. Architecture Decisions
- **Exactly 3 Agents**: Agent 1 (Quant), Agent 2 (Relationship/Intelligence), Agent 3 (Risk/Execution).
- **Relational Intelligence**: Uses DuckDB to strictly manage Entities, Exposures, Events, and Relationships.
- **Quantitative Time-Series**: Uses Parquet for OHLCV storage.
- **No Neo4j**: Graph databases are forbidden until proven necessary.
- **Strict Point-In-Time (PIT)**: All data queries must mathematically prevent future-data leakage using `valid_from` and `publication_time` boundaries.

## 6. Important Lessons Learned
1. **Specificity Must Survive Counterfactuals**: The Gold->ANTM thesis failed because the broad market also moved on that day. A hypothesis must prove it reacts *differently* than non-exposed peers.
2. **Correlation is not Causality**: Just because a stock moved does not mean the event caused it. The `UNKNOWN_COMPONENT` is a mandatory valid outcome for attribution.
3. **Specificity is an Observation, Not an Explanation**: A $\ge 2.5\%$ price divergence vs peers classifies a candidate as `COMPANY_SPECIFIC_CANDIDATE`, but attributing that move to a company catalyst requires independent contemporary PIT evidence.
4. **Contextual Sector Harmony Beats Isolated Breakouts**: Standalone breakouts often fail due to short-term mean reversion. Breakouts accompanied by contextual sector peer momentum (`SECTOR_WIDE`) exhibit sustained multi-day directional follow-through.
5. **Volume Acceleration is the Engine of Swing EV**: Technical score alone without volume expansion does not produce positive $H_{10}$ returns; $rel\_vol > 1.2$ transforms a flat setup into a $+2.20\%$ $H_{10}$ swing move.
6. **Beware Composite Gates vs Independent Alpha**: Multi-factor scores often duplicate upstream features. Always ablate composite metrics to identify the true underlying drivers (`AGENT3_COMPOSITE_DEPENDENCY`).
7. **Alpha is Sector-Conditional**: Breakout + Volume momentum functions robustly in cyclical commodity stocks ($H_5 = +1.81\%$), but is flat in financial/banking stocks ($H_5 = +0.03\%$). Do not assume universal market-wide transmission.
8. **Substrate Honesty**: Missing historical data must be classified as `NO_EVIDENCE_IN_DATASET` or `NOT_AVAILABLE`. Never backfill modern knowledge into historical decision points.
9. **Semantic Exposure Explains Signal Asymmetry**: Quantitative breakout signals in the IDX require underlying fundamental/commodity exposure networks to generate multi-day momentum; absent semantic exposure, technical breakouts mean-revert.
10. **Separate Predictive, Explanatory, and Governance Value**: Semantic reasoning does not add predictive lift over deterministic exposure ($\Delta EV = 0.00\%$), but its explanatory and governance functions are indispensable for institutional rigor and risk control.
11. **Common Factor Flow Dominates Gross Direction; Disclosures Drive Idiosyncratic Divergence**: Synchronized sector basket flow sets the general tide of returns, but contemporary primary disclosures significantly discriminate which stocks within the basket achieve positive excess returns against peers ($\Delta \text{Excess } H_5 = +3.10\%, p = 0.0300$).
12. **Absence of Record is Not Verified No-Event**: $\text{ABSENCE\_OF\_RECORD} \neq \text{VERIFIED\_NO\_EVENT}$. When disclosure sources are un-ingested, the absence of an event record reflects data missingness (`NO_EVIDENCE_IN_DATASET`), not proof that no event occurred.
13. **Raw Artifact First Guarantees Evidentiary Traceability**: Every historical semantic claim must be backed by a cryptographically hashed raw source artifact, preventing both LLM hallucination and post-hoc narrative fabrication.
14. **Unobserved is Not Genuine Low Disclosure**: $\text{NO\_EVIDENCE\_IN\_DATASET} \neq \text{VERIFIED\_NO\_EVENT} \neq \text{GENUINE\_LOW\_DISCLOSURE\_PERIOD}$. Un-ingested historical windows must never be asserted as "quiet periods" without affirmative source coverage proving the absence of material events.
15. **Integrity is Not Authenticity**: $\text{INTEGRITY\_VERIFIED (SHA-256)} \neq \text{AUTHENTICITY\_VERIFIED} \neq \text{PIT\_VERIFIED}$. A valid cryptographic hash only proves file uncorruptedness; verifying source authenticity requires proving document origin, official issuance timestamps, and cross-source corroboration.
