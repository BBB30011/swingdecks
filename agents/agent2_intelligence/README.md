# Agent 2: Market Intelligence & Red Team

Agent 2 acts as the fundamental, qualitative, and adversarial researcher. It receives candidates from Agent 1 (Quant) and subjects them to rigorous evidence-based scrutiny before passing them to Agent 3 (Investment Committee).

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Market Intelligence
- **Catalyst Tracking**: Evaluates the materiality and timeline of active catalysts (e.g., earnings, corporate actions, M&A).
- **Macro & Policy Integration**: Maps global factors, commodity prices, and policy shifts to specific sector and company exposures.
- **Fundamental Quality**: Validates structural company health.
- **Flow & Ownership Intelligence**: Incorporates Bandarmology/Flow states mapped against institutional footprint.

### 2. Red Team (Contradiction & Bear Case)
- **Adversarial Scrutiny**: Actively hunts for reasons *not* to trade the setup.
- **Contradiction Analysis**: If the price action is heavily bullish but the macroeconomic backdrop for the sector is collapsing, Agent 2 must highlight the `HIGH` severity contradiction.
- **Unknowns Tracking**: Explicitly lists missing information or "unknowns" that could present hidden risk.

## Core Constraints (Aligned with `AGENTS.md`)
- **No Confirmation Bias**: MUST NOT discard negative evidence just to support a bullish thesis.
- **Strict Point-In-Time (PIT)**: MUST ONLY use information strictly available at the `knowledge_available_at` timestamp. Forward-looking news or post-facto earnings reports cannot be used.
- **Immutability of Evidence**: MUST NOT revise historical evidence after observing the subsequent price action.
